# 1. Purpose

This document defines the major development milestones for the ShiftOS MVP.

Each milestone represents a complete business capability that can be demonstrated, tested and accepted before development proceeds to the next stage.

---

# 2. Objectives

Development milestones shall:

- Measure meaningful progress.
- Deliver usable functionality.
- Support incremental validation.
- Reduce project risk.
- Enable continuous demonstrations.

---

# 3. Milestone Principles

Every milestone shall:

- Deliver working software.
- Be independently testable.
- Include automated tests.
- Include documentation updates.
- Be deployable to the Staging environment.

Milestones are based on completed business capabilities rather than implementation effort.

---

# 4. Milestone 1 – Platform Foundation

Business capability:

- Organizations can be created.
- Managers can authenticate securely.
- Multi-tenant isolation is operational.
- Core infrastructure is functional.

Success criteria:

- Authentication complete.
- Authorization complete.
- Organization setup complete.
- CI/CD operational.

---

# 5. Milestone 2 – Employee Management

Business capability:

Managers can:

- Create employees.
- Edit employees.
- Invite employees.
- Manage employee records.

Employees can:

- Accept invitations.
- Activate accounts.
- Access their profile.

---

# 6. Milestone 3 – Shift Scheduling

Business capability:

Managers can:

- Create shifts.
- Publish schedules.
- Modify schedules.
- Resolve scheduling conflicts.

Employees can:

- View assigned shifts.

---

# 7. Milestone 4 – Attendance

Business capability:

Employees can:

- Clock in.
- Clock out.

Supervisors and managers can:

- Review attendance.
- Handle attendance exceptions.
- View attendance records.

---

# 8. Milestone 5 – Task Management

Business capability:

Managers and supervisors can:

- Create tasks.
- Assign tasks.
- Review completion.

Employees can:

- View assigned tasks.
- Complete assigned work.

---

# 9. Milestone 6 – Reporting

Business capability:

Managers can:

- View dashboards.
- Generate operational reports.
- Export payroll preparation reports.
- Review branch performance.

---

# 10. Milestone 7 – Production Readiness

Business capability:

The platform is ready for pilot customers.

Activities include:

- Performance validation.
- Security validation.
- Accessibility review.
- User acceptance testing.
- Documentation completion.
- Release preparation.

---

# 11. Milestone Reviews

At the completion of each milestone:

- Demonstrate completed functionality.
- Review acceptance criteria.
- Resolve critical issues.
- Update project documentation.
- Approve progression to the next milestone.

No milestone should begin until the previous milestone has been formally accepted.

---

# 12. Progress Measurement

Progress shall be measured using:

- Completed milestones.
- Acceptance status.
- Critical defects.
- Test pass rates.
- Documentation completion.

Percentage completion based solely on estimated effort shall not be used as the primary measure of progress.

# 14. Summary

The ShiftOS development milestones organize MVP delivery around complete business capabilities rather than technical tasks.

This approach provides clear progress measurement, supports continuous validation and ensures each completed milestone delivers meaningful value to stakeholders.
