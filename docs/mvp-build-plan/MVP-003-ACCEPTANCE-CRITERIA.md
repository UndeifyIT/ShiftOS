# 1. Purpose

This document defines the acceptance criteria that the ShiftOS MVP must satisfy before it is approved for pilot deployment.

Acceptance criteria verify that the MVP delivers complete, reliable and production-ready business capabilities.

---

# 2. Objectives

The MVP shall:

- Deliver agreed business functionality.
- Meet quality standards.
- Pass required testing.
- Demonstrate operational readiness.
- Be suitable for pilot customers.

---

# 3. Functional Acceptance

The following capabilities shall operate successfully:

### Authentication

- Users can authenticate securely.
- Password reset functions correctly.
- Session management operates correctly.
- Authorization is enforced.

---

### Organization Management

Managers can:

- Create organizations.
- Configure branches.
- Manage organization settings.

---

### Employee Management

Managers can:

- Create employees.
- Edit employee records.
- Invite employees.
- Deactivate employees.

Employees can:

- Accept invitations.
- Access their accounts.
- Maintain profile information.

---

### Shift Management

Managers can:

- Create shifts.
- Publish schedules.
- Modify shifts.
- Resolve scheduling conflicts.

Employees can view published schedules.

---

### Attendance

Employees can:

- Clock in.
- Clock out.

Managers and supervisors can:

- Review attendance.
- Correct attendance records where authorized.
- View attendance history.

---

### Task Management

Managers and supervisors can:

- Create tasks.
- Assign tasks.
- Review completion status.

Employees can:

- View assigned tasks.
- Complete assigned tasks.

---

### Reporting

Managers can:

- View dashboards.
- Generate operational reports.
- Export payroll preparation reports.

---

# 4. Non-Functional Acceptance

The MVP shall satisfy:

- Security requirements.
- Performance requirements.
- Accessibility requirements.
- Reliability requirements.
- Multi-tenant isolation requirements.

---

# 5. Quality Acceptance

The following shall be completed successfully:

- Unit testing.
- Integration testing.
- End-to-end testing.
- Security testing.
- Performance testing.
- User Acceptance Testing.

Critical defects shall be resolved prior to approval.

---

# 6. Documentation Acceptance

Required documentation shall include:

- Architecture.
- Database design.
- API specifications.
- UI specifications.
- Deployment procedures.
- Operational procedures.

Documentation shall reflect the implemented system.

---

# 7. Operational Acceptance

The platform shall demonstrate:

- Successful deployment.
- Monitoring.
- Logging.
- Error tracking.
- Backup procedures.
- Rollback capability.

Operational tooling shall be validated before launch.

---

# 8. Business Acceptance

Representative business users shall confirm that:

- Core workflows support daily operations.
- Business rules are correctly enforced.
- User experience is acceptable.
- Required reports are available.

Formal UAT sign-off is required.

---

# 9. Release Approval

The MVP shall not be approved unless:

- Acceptance criteria are satisfied.
- Outstanding issues have been reviewed.
- Critical defects are resolved.
- Required approvals are documented.

Approval shall be recorded as part of the release process.

---

# 10. Exit Criteria

The MVP is considered complete when:

- All mandatory business capabilities are operational.
- Required testing has passed.
- Documentation is complete.
- Production deployment is approved.
- Pilot customers can operate successfully without engineering intervention for normal daily workflows.

# 12. Summary

The ShiftOS MVP acceptance criteria define the functional, non-functional, operational and business requirements that must be satisfied before pilot deployment.
