**Status:** Living Document

**Classification:** Governance

**Owner:** ShiftOS Product Team

---

# Purpose

This document outlines the long-term direction of ShiftOS beyond the Minimum Viable Product (MVP).

Unlike the MVP Scope, this document does not represent committed work. Instead, it captures the strategic vision for how the platform may evolve over time.

The roadmap is intentionally organized into capability phases rather than dates or release versions. This allows priorities to adapt based on customer feedback, product adoption, and business needs without requiring constant restructuring.

Items in this roadmap should be treated as opportunities rather than promises.

---

# Roadmap Principles

The ShiftOS roadmap follows these principles:

- Customer problems drive priorities.
- Stability comes before new features.
- Simplicity is preferred over feature quantity.
- Every major capability must support the product vision.
- Features may move between phases as priorities evolve.
- Shipping a smaller, high-quality release is preferable to delaying for additional functionality.

# Phase 1 — MVP Foundation

**Status:** In Progress

The primary objective of this phase is to deliver a secure, reliable and production-ready workforce operations platform.

Core capabilities include:

- Organization management
- Multi-tenant architecture
- Branch management
- User authentication
- Role-based permissions
- Employee management
- Shift scheduling
- Attendance tracking
- Task management
- Announcements
- Notifications
- Reporting dashboards
- Payroll preparation exports
- Shifty (Guidance & Recommendations)
- Progressive Web App (PWA)

**Success Criteria**

- Businesses can operate daily workforce activities using ShiftOS without relying on multiple disconnected tools.

# Roadmap Governance

A feature should only move from this roadmap into active development when:

- The business problem has been validated.
- The feature aligns with the product vision.
- Customer demand has been demonstrated.
- Technical feasibility has been assessed.
- Dependencies have been identified.
- Required specifications have been completed.
- The Decision Log has been updated.

# Summary

The ShiftOS roadmap represents the long-term evolution of the platform.

It is designed to provide strategic direction while remaining flexible enough to adapt to changing customer needs and business priorities.

The roadmap is intentionally capability-driven rather than date-driven, ensuring that development is guided by value, readiness and evidence instead of arbitrary release schedules.

---

# Strategic Exclusions

The following capabilities are intentionally outside the long-term strategic direction of ShiftOS.

These exclusions exist to maintain product focus and prevent the platform from evolving into a general-purpose business management suite.

Unless the core product vision is formally revised, ShiftOS should integrate with specialist solutions rather than implement these capabilities directly.

## Finance & Accounting

ShiftOS will not become:

- An accounting platform
- A bookkeeping system
- An invoicing platform
- A tax management system
- A budgeting application
- A financial reporting suite
- A banking platform

---

## Payroll Processing

ShiftOS will not:

- Calculate employee salaries
- Process payroll
- Generate payslips
- Handle tax deductions
- Process pension contributions
- Transfer employee payments

ShiftOS will continue to provide accurate attendance records, hours worked, overtime summaries and payroll-ready exports for external payroll systems.

---

## Human Resources (HR)

ShiftOS will not become a complete Human Resources Information System (HRIS).

This includes:

- Recruitment
- Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)
- Candidate management
- Interview scheduling
- Employee performance reviews
- Compensation management
- Benefits administration
- Learning Management Systems (LMS)
- Employee training platforms
- Succession planning

---

## Customer Management

ShiftOS will not become a Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform.

This includes:

- Customer databases
- Sales pipelines
- Marketing automation
- Customer support ticketing
- Lead management
- Customer communication platforms

---

## Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)

ShiftOS will not replace ERP systems.

Excluded capabilities include:

- Inventory management
- Procurement
- Supply chain management
- Manufacturing execution
- Warehouse inventory control
- Order management
- Asset management
- Financial operations

---

## General Collaboration

ShiftOS communication features exist solely to support workforce operations.

The platform will not become:

- A team chat application
- A social network
- A video conferencing platform
- A document collaboration platform
- A file sharing service

---

## Content & Media Management

ShiftOS will not provide:

- Cloud file storage
- Digital asset management
- Media libraries
- Content management systems
- Website builders
- Blog publishing
- Knowledge base platforms

---

## General Project Management

Task management within ShiftOS is operational in nature.

The platform will not evolve into:

- Agile project management software
- Sprint planning tools
- Product roadmap software
- Portfolio management systems
- Software development lifecycle tools

---

## Consumer Applications

ShiftOS is built for organizations, not consumers.

The platform will not include:

- Consumer scheduling tools
- Personal productivity applications
- Family calendars
- Lifestyle management
- Personal to-do applications

---

## Strategic Principle

Whenever a proposed feature overlaps with one of the exclusions above, the preferred solution is to integrate with an established specialist platform rather than expand the functional scope of ShiftOS.

Every new capability should strengthen ShiftOS's position as a workforce operations platform.

If a feature does not directly improve workforce planning, workforce coordination, workforce visibility or workforce intelligence, it should generally remain outside the long-term product strategy.
A strategic exclusion may only be reconsidered if the ShiftOS Vision document is formally updated.
