# Introduction

This document defines all official ShiftOS terminology beginning with the letter **E**.

These definitions form part of the official ShiftOS vocabulary and must be used consistently throughout specifications, software development, documentation, testing, database design and user interfaces.

---

# Email

## Business Definition

Email is the primary communication channel used by ShiftOS during the MVP for identity verification, account invitations, password resets and important system notifications.

---

## Technical Definition

Emails are generated by backend services and delivered asynchronously through the configured email provider.

---

## Business Context

Email is used for:

- Organization invitations
- Supervisor invitations
- Employee invitations
- Password reset
- Email verification
- Security notifications

---

## Used By

- Managers
- Supervisors
- Employees
- System

---

## Related Specifications

- USR-004 Invitations
- USR-005 Email Verification
- USR-006 Password Reset
- INT-002 Email

---

## Related Terms

- Invitation
- Verification
- Notification

---

## Notes

Email is the only supported invitation channel in the MVP.

WhatsApp and SMS are planned for future releases.

---

# Employee

## Business Definition

An Employee is an individual who performs work for an organization and is managed within ShiftOS.

Employees are the primary workforce managed by the platform.

---

## Technical Definition

An Employee is represented by an employee record linked to a user account, organization and at least one branch.

---

## Business Context

Employees can:

- Receive shifts
- Clock in
- Clock out
- Receive announcements
- Complete tasks
- View schedules
- Manage their profile

Employees cannot create schedules or manage other employees.

---

## Used By

- Managers
- Supervisors
- Employees

---

## Related Specifications

- EMP-001 Employee Profile
- EMP-007 Employee Lifecycle

---

## Related Terms

- Supervisor
- Manager
- User
- Position

---

## Notes

An employee represents a business role, not merely an authenticated user.

---

# Employee ID

## Business Definition

An Employee ID is the unique identifier assigned to an employee within an organization.

---

## Technical Definition

The Employee ID is distinct from the internal database primary key and may follow organization-specific formatting.

---

## Business Context

Employee IDs are commonly used for:

- Internal reference
- Printed reports
- Payroll preparation
- Staff identification

---

## Used By

- Managers
- Supervisors

---

## Related Specifications

- EMP-001 Employee Profile

---

## Related Terms

- Employee
- Organization

---

## Notes

Employee IDs must be unique within an organization.

Different organizations may reuse the same Employee ID format.

---

# Employment

## Business Definition

Employment describes the formal working relationship between an employee and an organization.

---

## Technical Definition

Employment includes the employee's status, branch assignments, position and employment history.

---

## Business Context

Employment begins when an employee joins the organization and ends when employment is terminated or archived.

---

## Used By

- Managers

---

## Related Specifications

- EMP-002 Employment Status
- EMP-005 Employment History

---

## Related Terms

- Employee
- Position
- Branch Assignment

---

# Employment History

## Business Definition

Employment History records significant events throughout an employee's time within an organization.

---

## Technical Definition

Historical records are immutable operational events associated with an employee profile.

---

## Business Context

Examples include:

- Hire date
- Position changes
- Branch transfers
- Status changes
- Termination

---

## Used By

- Managers

---

## Related Specifications

- EMP-005 Employment History

---

## Related Terms

- Employee
- Employment
- Status

---

## Notes

Historical records must never be deleted.

---

# Employment Status

## Business Definition

Employment Status indicates whether an employee is currently eligible to participate in operational activities.

---

## Technical Definition

Employment status is represented using controlled status values.

Examples include:

- Active
- Suspended
- Archived

---

## Business Context

Employment status determines whether an employee:

- Can receive shifts
- Can receive tasks
- Can clock in
- Can access the application

---

## Used By

- Managers
- Supervisors

---

## Related Specifications

- EMP-002 Employment Status

---

## Related Terms

- Employee
- Active Employee
- Archived Employee

# Error

## Business Definition

An Error is a condition that prevents a requested operation from completing successfully.

---

## Technical Definition

Errors are categorized, logged and returned using standardized backend responses.

---

## Business Context

Examples include:

- Permission denied
- Validation failed
- Shift conflict
- Invalid attendance state

---

## Used By

- Entire Platform

---

## Related Specifications

- API-006 Error Handling

---

## Related Terms

- Exception
- Validation
- Conflict

---

## Notes

Errors should always provide meaningful feedback without exposing sensitive system information.

# Export

## Business Definition

An Export is a generated file containing operational data for external use.

---

## Technical Definition

Exports are generated by backend reporting services using standardized file formats.

---

## Business Context

Examples include:

- Attendance Report
- Payroll Preparation
- Shift Schedule
- Employee Directory

---

## Used By

- Managers

---

## Related Specifications

- REP-008 Data Exports

---

## Related Terms

- Report
- CSV
- Excel

---

## Notes

Exports are read-only representations of existing data and must never modify operational records.

# Expiry

## Business Definition

Expiry refers to the point at which a resource or permission is no longer valid.

---

## Technical Definition

Expiry may apply to authentication sessions, invitation links, password reset links or temporary access tokens.

---

## Business Context

Examples include:

- Invitation expires after a defined period.
- Password reset link expires.
- User session expires after inactivity.

---

## Used By

- System

---

## Related Specifications

- USR-004 Invitations
- USR-006 Password Reset
- USR-007 Session Management

---

## Related Terms

- Session
- Authentication
- Token
