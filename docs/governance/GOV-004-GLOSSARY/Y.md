# Introduction

Only terms that have practical relevance to the ShiftOS platform are included.

---

# YAML (YAML Ain't Markup Language)

## Business Definition

YAML is a human-readable configuration format commonly used to define application settings and deployment workflows.

---

## Technical Definition

Within the ShiftOS ecosystem, YAML may be used for:

- CI/CD pipeline definitions
- Infrastructure configuration
- Deployment workflows
- GitHub Actions
- Development tooling

YAML is not used as a primary application data format.

---

## Related Specifications

- OPS-002 CI/CD

---

## Related Terms

- Configuration
- Deployment
- GitHub Actions

# Year

## Business Definition

A Year is a calendar period used for long-term reporting, planning and historical analysis.

---

## Technical Definition

Yearly periods may be used when calculating:

- Annual attendance statistics
- Workforce growth
- Employee retention
- Business performance
- Operational KPIs

All yearly calculations must respect the organization's configured timezone.

---

## Related Specifications

- REP-001 Reporting Philosophy

---

## Related Terms

- Month
- Week
- Reporting

---

# Year-to-Date (YTD)

## Business Definition

Year-to-Date (YTD) represents all recorded business activity from the beginning of the current calendar year up to the present day.

---

## Technical Definition

YTD reporting may include:

- Hours worked
- Attendance rates
- Shift counts
- Completed tasks
- Operational KPIs

YTD calculations are generated dynamically from historical records.

---

## Related Specifications

- REP-007 Dashboard Metrics

---

## Related Terms

- KPI
- Analytics
- Reporting

---

# Yield

## Business Definition

Yield refers to the amount of useful output produced from a given process or resource.

---

## Technical Definition

ShiftOS does not currently calculate operational yield directly.

The term may appear in future analytics modules or integrations where productivity metrics are derived from external systems.

---

## Business Context

Examples outside the MVP include:

- Warehouse picking yield
- Manufacturing output yield
- Production efficiency

Yield is documented for future extensibility only.

---

## Related Terms

- Productivity
- Analytics

# Summary

The letter **Y** contains only a limited number of terms relevant to ShiftOS. These definitions primarily support infrastructure configuration and long-term reporting concepts while leaving room for future enterprise analytics capabilities.
