# Purpose

This document defines the initial pricing strategy for ShiftOS.

The purpose of pricing is to create a sustainable SaaS business while remaining accessible to shift-based businesses in Nigeria and similar emerging markets.

Pricing should reflect:

- Customer value.
- Business size.
- Operational complexity.
- Product maturity.
- Ability to expand.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

ShiftOS should not compete as the cheapest workforce tool.

The pricing philosophy is:

> **Affordable enough for growing businesses, valuable enough to become a critical operational system.**

The goal is to create a product customers rely on daily, not a low-cost tool that is easily replaced.

---

# Pricing Model Recommendation

## Primary Model

ShiftOS should use:

**Subscription pricing based on organization size and workforce capacity.**

The subscription includes:

- Number of employees.
- Number of branches.
- Feature access.
- Support level.

---

# Why Not Pure Per Employee Pricing?

Pure per-employee pricing creates problems:

- Businesses fear growth because costs increase immediately.
- Employee numbers fluctuate.
- Scheduling tools are often used more by managers than employees.
- Small businesses prefer predictable monthly expenses.

---

# Why Not Flat Unlimited Pricing?

Unlimited pricing creates problems:

- Large organizations consume more resources.
- Small businesses subsidize larger customers.
- Limits future scalability.

# Recommended Pricing Structure

Examples:

- Small restaurants.
- Small retail stores.
- Single-branch businesses.

---

### Price

**₦15,000/month**

or

**₦150,000/year**

---

### Limits

Includes:

- Up to 25 employees.
- 1 branch.
- Manager access.
- Supervisor access.
- Employee accounts.

---

### Features

Includes:

- Employee profiles.
- Basic employee directory.
- Branch setup.

## Scheduling

- Shift creation.
- Shift assignment.
- Schedule viewing.

## Attendance

- Clock-in.
- Clock-out.
- Attendance history.

## Communication

- Announcements.
- Basic notifications.

### Purpose

Convert businesses currently using:

- WhatsApp.
- Spreadsheets.

# Pricing Mistakes To Avoid

## Competing Only On Price

Low prices can attract customers who do not value the product.

---

## Too Many Plans

Complex pricing creates confusion.

---

## Charging For Every Small Feature

Customers should feel they are buying a complete solution.

---

## Pricing Before Product Validation

Early pricing should remain flexible while learning from customers.

# Summary

ShiftOS should use a subscription model designed for growing shift-based businesses.

The initial strategy is to provide accessible entry pricing while creating a clear upgrade path as customers add employees, branches and operational complexity.

The goal is not to be the cheapest workforce tool.

The goal is to become an essential operational platform that customers are willing to pay for every month.
