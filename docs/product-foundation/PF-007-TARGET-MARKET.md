# Purpose

This document defines the target market for ShiftOS.

It identifies the businesses, industries and customer segments that ShiftOS is designed to serve.

A clear target market ensures that product development, marketing, sales strategy and customer decisions remain focused on organizations that experience the workforce operational problems ShiftOS is built to solve.

---

# Market Definition

ShiftOS targets organizations that rely on shift-based employees to operate their businesses.

These organizations typically have:

- Employees working scheduled shifts.
- Multiple employees requiring coordination.
- Managers responsible for workforce planning.
- Daily operational tasks.
- Attendance tracking requirements.
- Communication needs across teams.
- A need for better operational visibility.

The core market is businesses where workforce coordination is a critical part of daily operations.

---

# Primary Target Market

## Small and Medium-Sized Shift-Based Businesses

The primary target market for ShiftOS is small and medium-sized businesses that have moved beyond informal workforce management but do not require complex enterprise workforce systems.

Typical characteristics:

- 10–500 employees.
- 1–50 branches or operational locations.
- Multiple shifts per day.
- Managers or supervisors coordinating teams.
- Growing operational complexity.
- Reliance on spreadsheets, messaging apps or manual processes.

These businesses represent the strongest initial fit because they experience significant operational pain while often lacking suitable workforce technology.

# Geographic Market Strategy

## Initial Market Focus

ShiftOS will initially focus on markets where:

- Shift-based businesses are common.
- Manual workforce processes are widespread.
- Digital transformation adoption is increasing.
- Businesses need affordable operational software.

The initial focus is expected to include:

- Nigeria
- Other African markets
- Emerging markets with similar operational challenges

---

# Expansion Market

After achieving product maturity, ShiftOS can expand into:

- United Kingdom
- United States
- Europe
- Middle East
- Other international markets

Expansion should occur after:

- Product-market fit is validated.
- Security requirements are mature.
- Compliance requirements are addressed.
- Enterprise capabilities are developed.

# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

The ideal ShiftOS customer has:

## Business Characteristics

- Operates using employee shifts.
- Has recurring workforce scheduling needs.
- Has multiple employees.
- Experiences operational coordination problems.
- Wants to replace manual processes.

---

## Operational Characteristics

The customer:

- Creates schedules regularly.
- Needs attendance visibility.
- Has supervisors or managers.
- Communicates operational updates frequently.
- Assigns recurring work.
- Requires accountability.

---

## Technology Characteristics

The customer:

- Uses smartphones or computers.
- Is comfortable adopting cloud software.
- Wants operational improvement.
- Does not have a suitable workforce operations platform.

# Market Problems ShiftOS Does Not Primarily Target

ShiftOS is not initially designed for:

## Large Enterprise Corporations

Reason:

Large enterprises often require:

- Complex HR integrations.
- Custom compliance workflows.
- Advanced workforce analytics.
- Enterprise procurement processes.

These capabilities may be addressed later.

---

## Businesses Without Shift Workers

Reason:

The core value proposition depends on managing scheduled workforce operations.

---

## Individuals Managing Personal Tasks

Reason:

ShiftOS is a business workforce operations platform, not a personal productivity tool.

---

# Market Positioning

ShiftOS positions itself between:

## Basic Manual Tools

Examples:

- Spreadsheets
- Paper schedules
- Messaging applications

Problems:

- Limited visibility.
- High manual effort.
- Poor accountability.

---

## Enterprise Workforce Platforms

Problems:

- Expensive.
- Complex.
- Difficult for smaller organizations.

---

## ShiftOS Position

A modern workforce operations platform that provides enterprise-quality operational capabilities with the simplicity and accessibility needed by growing shift-based businesses.

---

# Market Selection Principles

ShiftOS should prioritize markets that:

1. Have frequent workforce scheduling needs.
2. Experience operational coordination problems.
3. Have clear willingness to pay.
4. Can adopt cloud software.
5. Benefit from operational visibility.
6. Match existing product capabilities.

# Summary

ShiftOS is designed for organizations whose success depends on managing shift-based employees.

The initial focus is small and medium-sized businesses in industries such as restaurants, retail, hospitality, warehouses and similar operational environments.

By focusing on businesses with real workforce coordination challenges, ShiftOS can build a product that solves meaningful problems before expanding into broader markets.
