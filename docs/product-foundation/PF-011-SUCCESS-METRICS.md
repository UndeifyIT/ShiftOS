---

# Purpose

This document defines how ShiftOS measures success.

Success metrics provide a framework for evaluating:

- Product effectiveness.
- Customer value.
- User adoption.
- Operational improvement.
- Business sustainability.

Metrics should guide decisions rather than simply measure activity.

---

# Success Philosophy

ShiftOS does not measure success by the number of features released or users registered.

The primary question is:

> **Is ShiftOS helping businesses operate their workforce more effectively?**

A successful ShiftOS customer should experience:

- Less administrative work.
- Better workforce visibility.
- Fewer operational mistakes.
- Improved communication.
- More reliable workforce management.

---

# Metric Categories

ShiftOS success metrics are divided into five categories:

1. Product Adoption Metrics
2. Customer Value Metrics
3. Operational Improvement Metrics
4. Business Growth Metrics
5. Platform Health Metrics

---

# Category 1 — Product Adoption Metrics

These metrics measure whether users are actively adopting ShiftOS.

---

# Active Organizations

## Definition

The number of organizations actively using ShiftOS within a defined period.

---

## Why It Matters

A registered organization does not indicate value.

Active usage indicates customers are relying on ShiftOS for operations.

---

## Measurement Examples

Track:

- Daily active organizations.
- Weekly active organizations.
- Monthly active organizations.

---

# Active Users

## Definition

The number of users regularly interacting with ShiftOS.

Measured across:

- Managers.
- Supervisors.
- Employees.

---

## Why It Matters

Workforce platforms require participation across multiple roles.

---

# Feature Adoption

## Definition

The percentage of customers using important product capabilities.

Examples:

- Scheduling.
- Attendance.
- Tasks.
- Announcements.
- Reporting.

---

## Why It Matters

A feature that exists but is unused does not create customer value.

---

# Workflow Completion Rate

## Definition

The percentage of users who successfully complete important workflows.

Examples:

- Creating a shift.
- Assigning employees.
- Clocking attendance.
- Completing tasks.

---

## Why It Matters

Completion rate identifies friction in user experiences.

---

# Category 2 — Customer Value Metrics

These measure whether customers receive meaningful benefits.

---

# Administrative Time Reduction

## Definition

The reduction in time customers spend managing workforce operations.

Examples:

Before ShiftOS:

- Manual scheduling.
- Spreadsheet updates.
- Attendance reconciliation.

After ShiftOS:

- Automated workflows.
- Centralized information.
- Faster management.

---

# Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)

## Definition

A measure of customer satisfaction after interactions or usage experiences.

---

## Why It Matters

Satisfied customers are more likely to continue using ShiftOS.

---

# Net Promoter Score (NPS)

## Definition

Measures how likely customers are to recommend ShiftOS.

---

## Why It Matters

Strong recommendations indicate customers see meaningful value.

---

# Customer Retention

## Definition

The percentage of customers who continue using ShiftOS over time.

---

## Why It Matters

Retention is one of the strongest indicators of product-market fit.

---

# Category 3 — Workforce Operational Metrics

These measure the operational problems ShiftOS helps improve.

---

# Scheduling Efficiency

## Definition

Measures how effectively customers create and manage schedules.

Possible measurements:

- Time required to create schedules.
- Number of scheduling corrections.
- Shift conflicts prevented.

---

# Attendance Accuracy

## Definition

Measures improvement in workforce attendance tracking.

Possible measurements:

- Reduction in attendance disputes.
- More complete attendance records.
- Faster attendance reporting.

---

# Shift Reliability

## Definition

Measures how reliably planned shifts are completed.

Possible measurements:

- Missed shifts.
- Last-minute changes.
- Coverage issues.

---

# Task Completion Rate

## Definition

Measures completion of assigned operational tasks.

Possible measurements:

- Completed tasks.
- Late tasks.
- Unverified tasks.

---

# Communication Effectiveness

## Definition

Measures whether important operational communication reaches employees.

Possible measurements:

- Announcement views.
- Acknowledgements.
- Response rates.

---

# Category 4 — Business Growth Metrics

These measure ShiftOS as a SaaS business.

---

# Customer Acquisition

## Definition

The number of new organizations adopting ShiftOS.

---

## Measurement Examples

- New organizations per month.
- Conversion rates.
- Sales pipeline.

---

# Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

## Definition

The expected value generated from a customer during their relationship with ShiftOS.

---

# Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

## Definition

The cost required to acquire a new customer.

---

# Revenue Growth

## Definition

Measures growth in recurring revenue.

Future metrics:

- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR).
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR).

---

# Expansion Revenue

## Definition

Revenue generated when customers grow their ShiftOS usage.

Examples:

- Adding more branches.
- Adding more employees.
- Upgrading plans.

---

# Category 5 — Platform Health Metrics

These measure technical quality.

---

# System Reliability

## Definition

The availability and stability of ShiftOS.

Important measurements:

- Uptime.
- Error rates.
- Service interruptions.

---

# Performance

## Definition

Measures how quickly users can complete actions.

Examples:

- Page loading times.
- Mobile responsiveness.
- Database response times.

---

# Security Health

## Definition

Measures protection of customer data.

Measurements include:

- Security incidents.
- Failed authentication attempts.
- Permission violations.
- Audit events.

---

# Support Health

## Definition

Measures customer support effectiveness.

Metrics:

- Response times.
- Issue resolution times.
- Common problem categories.

---

# MVP Success Metrics

During MVP, ShiftOS should prioritize validation over scale.

The most important MVP indicators are:

## 1. Organizations Successfully Using ShiftOS

The product must prove real businesses can operate with it.

---

## 2. Weekly Active Managers

Managers are the operational owners.

---

## 3. Successful Shift Creation

Scheduling is a core workflow.

---

## 4. Employee Adoption

Employees must actually use the system.

---

## 5. Reduction Of Manual Processes

Customers should report measurable improvement.

---

## 6. Customer Retention

Early customers continuing usage is a stronger signal than initial signups.

---

# Metrics We Should Avoid Optimizing Too Early

ShiftOS should avoid focusing heavily on:

## Total Signups

Many users may never become active customers.

---

## Number Of Features

Feature quantity does not equal product value.

---

## Downloads

Downloads do not indicate operational adoption.

---

## Screen Views

Usage without meaningful outcomes is not success.

---

## Product Team

- Adoption.
- Usability.
- Customer value.

---

## Engineering Team

- Security.

---

## Business Team

- Revenue.

- Retention.

# Governance

Success metrics should be reviewed regularly.

Changes should consider:

- Business stage.
- Customer feedback.
- Product maturity.
- Strategic priorities.

Metrics should evolve as ShiftOS grows but should always remain connected to customer value.

---

# Summary

ShiftOS success is defined by helping businesses manage shift workers more effectively.

The strongest indicators are not simply growth numbers, but whether organizations rely on ShiftOS daily, experience operational improvements and continue receiving measurable value from the platform.
